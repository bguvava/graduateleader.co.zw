Our Impact Areas

Six Pillars of Measurable Change

SGLD's impact is felt across six interconnected dimensions — each contributing to Zimbabwe's sustainable economic growth and social development.

Graduate Self-Development & Skills

Equipping graduates with practical, industry-relevant competencies that build confidence, professional identity, and lifelong learning habits that extend far beyond any single programme.

92% of graduates report significant skill improvement

High Graduate Employability

Creating real, measurable employment outcomes by linking graduates with industry, building workplace readiness, and facilitating direct connections between talent and opportunity.

78% of SGLD alumni employed within 6 months

Industry-Specific Skills Development

Delivering specialised training in high-demand sectors — IT, AI, finance, foreign languages, SHE compliance — addressing critical skills shortages that constrain economic growth.

85% of corporates report measurable team improvement

Enhanced Research Outputs

Bridging academia and industry through applied research, knowledge exchange, and collaborative R&D projects that generate insights with real-world policy and business applications.

Active research partnerships with institutions & corporates

More Successful Entrepreneurs

From idea to enterprise — SGLD's business incubation programme has supported the launch and growth of over 30 businesses, creating jobs and catalysing economic activity across Zimbabwe.

88% of incubated businesses operational after 12 months

New Business Opportunities

Opening doors through strategic partnerships, matchmaking between graduates and industry, and international connections that create new markets, collaborations, and commercial opportunities.

70+ new business connections facilitated
