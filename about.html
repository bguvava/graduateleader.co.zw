About SGLD

A specialist post-graduate institution built to bridge academia and industry — for Zimbabwe, and for the world.

Our Story

Born From a National Need

The School of Graduate Leadership Development was incorporated in Zimbabwe in 2023 in direct response to one of the country's most pressing socio-economic challenges — graduate unemployment. Despite graduating thousands of young professionals annually, Zimbabwe's universities and colleges were producing graduates who lacked the practical, industry-relevant skills that employers actually needed.

SGLD was founded to close that gap. Operating under the philosophy of lifelong learning, we exist not as a degree-awarding institution, but as a dynamic skills-development and capacity-building powerhouse that bridges the worlds of academia and industry.

Purpose & Direction

Mission & Vision

Our Mission
Equipping Leaders for Today's Challenges

To bridge the widening gap between academic institutions and industry by equipping graduates, corporates, and institutions with practical competencies, leadership skills, and industry-specific knowledge — nurturing innovation, supporting business incubation, and forging strategic partnerships locally and internationally.

Our Vision
Africa's Premier Graduate Development Hub

To become the most impactful post-graduate professional development institution in Africa — recognised for transforming graduates into industry leaders, catalysing entrepreneurship, and building the human capital that drives sustainable economic growth across the continent.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Every programme, partnership, and interaction at SGLD is governed by these foundational principles.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standards in every programme, interaction, and outcome — excellence is not optional, it is our baseline.

Innovation

We embrace new ideas, technologies, and approaches that challenge the status quo and create better pathways for graduates and organisations.

Our Journey

SGLD Milestones

Q1 2023
SGLD Incorporated

School of Graduate Leadership Development officially incorporated in Zimbabwe with a mandate to bridge the gap between academia and industry.

Q2 2023
First Training Cohort

Launched our inaugural capacity building and life skills programmes, welcoming our first cohort of graduates from universities across Harare.

Q3 2023
Corporate Partnerships Established

Secured first corporate training contracts and consultancy agreements with leading Zimbabwean organisations across multiple sectors.

Q4 2023
Virtual Training Platform Launch

Expanded delivery to include Live Virtual Training, enabling graduates across Zimbabwe and internationally to access our programmes remotely.

2024
Business Incubation Programme

Launched dedicated business incubation support, mentorship networks, and entrepreneurship tracks — celebrating first batch of incubated businesses.

2024 – Present
International Partnerships & Expansion

Forged strategic alliances with NSSA, ISO bodies, ACCA partners, and international institutions — expanding reach to 6+ countries across Africa and beyond.
