Service 04

Capacity Building Delivery Modes

Four flexible training delivery modes — each designed to fit your schedule, location, and learning preferences.

01

Classroom Training

Immersive face-to-face learning in our Harare training centre. Structured, facilitated sessions with peer interaction, group activities, and real-time expert guidance — ideal for intensive programmes and collaborative learning.

02

Live Virtual Training

Expert-led sessions delivered globally via secure, high-quality video conferencing platforms. Participate from anywhere in the world without sacrificing the interactivity and real-time feedback of a live classroom experience.

03

Corporate Group Training

Customised training delivered directly to your organisation's team — in your workplace or a venue of your choice. Programmes are tailored to your industry, team challenges, and specific learning objectives.

04

Focused 1-to-1 Training

Personalised, one-on-one coaching and mentorship sessions with an expert facilitator. Ideal for executives, professionals seeking targeted skill acceleration, or those requiring flexible scheduling around busy careers.
